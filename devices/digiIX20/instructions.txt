Step 1: Connect IX20 to 9-30 V DC power source
Step 2: Connect IX20 to PC with Ethernet cable in ETH2 port
Step 3: Select IX20 as device.
Step 4: Select excel sheet that corresponds to the airport.
Step 5: Select the gate.
Step 6: Click into the text box. 
Step 7: Scan the QR code or barcode on the device to input the device password and info.
Step 8: Hit submit.
Step 9: Hit Program Device.
